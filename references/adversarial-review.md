# Adversarial editing review — simulate a skeptical reader

Ask: **Could someone challenge any specific detail in the edited draft?**
- Did the rewrite invent a story, number, customer quote, founder motivation, internal strategy or causal result?
- Is the author presenting a public case as a firsthand account?
- Are `〜に違いない`, `間違いなく`, `多くの人` or `当然` stronger than source?
- Does `しかし`, `そのため`, `したがって` insert causal or contrast reasoning absent from input?
- Is `〜気がします` weakening a fact, or `〜です` turning an assumption into certainty?
- Were honorifics weakened to an impolite B2B tone, or did 尊敬語 and 謙譲語 swap actor roles?
- Did an elegant headline hide conditions or denominators (e.g., campaign scope, period, survey population)?
- Did everything get artificially colloquial? Does each paragraph end with `なんですよね`?
- Is the draft using a copied sentence or distinct lexical footprint from a linked benchmark author? Rewrite independently.
- Would the edited note read as a confession/success story even though source was a third-party research summary?

**Resolution:** If factual risk, HOLD; otherwise choose the least intrusive correction. No AI-detection or engagement score claims.
