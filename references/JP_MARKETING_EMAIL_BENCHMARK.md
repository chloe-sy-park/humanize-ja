# 日本語マーケティングコピー・広告・ビジネスメール — research note

Research cutoff: **2026-10-25**. Sources are live official/industry webpages; this is a *qualitative editorial crosswalk*, not a survey of Japanese consumer preferences. Descriptions are paraphrased rather than reproducing templates or article body text.

## Official primary sources / observed guardrails
1. 消費者庁 — 優良誤認: quality/value may mislead even if not deliberately false. https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/representation_regulation/misleading_representation/
2. 消費者庁 — 有利誤認: false discounts, fees or purchase conditions can mislead. https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/representation_regulation/advantageous_misidentification/
3. 消費者庁 — 二重価格表示: comparative pricing must have substantiated historical comparator and correct context. https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/representation_regulation/double_price/
4. 消費者庁 — ステルスマーケティングに関するQ&A: relevant control and relationship affect whether disclosure is required; commercial character must be recognizable. https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/faq/stealth_marketing
5. 消費者庁 — 打消し表示の実態調査: visual proximity/legibility matter, including mobile and video. https://www.caa.go.jp/policies/policy/representation/fair_labeling/survey
6. 厚生労働省 — 医薬品等の広告規制: false/exaggerated efficacy/performance claims are regulated by product class. https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/seisakunitsuite/bunya/kenkou_iryou/iyakuhin/koukokukisei/index.html
7. 日本データ通信協会 — 特定電子メール法: advertising-mail recipient consent is a default with exceptions and sender information/opt-out obligations. https://www.dekyo.or.jp/soudan/contents/taisaku/1-2.html

8. SHANON (2026-10-07), 件名: clear action/topic in subject; don't abuse urgency or bracket labels; adjust for known/unknown sender. https://www.shanon.co.jp/blog/entry/business_mail/
9. マネーフォワード (2026-03-18), 依頼メール: recipient, action, deadline, short background and respect for burden. https://biz.moneyforward.com/work-efficiency/basic/17505/

## Extracted editing hypotheses (not established causal rules)
- A strong Japanese marketing statement can be restrained or energetic; clarity of benefit and proof matter more than a globally applied 'Japanese modesty' assumption.
- Reader needs the truth of price / conditions and ability to take action; optimizing headline alone can lose consistency with LP.
- Japanese emails can be highly polite yet vague; purpose + action + relevant deadline improve practical clarity, **not a claim of quantified reply lift**.
- Vocabulary such as `ぜひ`, `恐れ入ります`, `革新的`, `いただけますと幸い` is not intrinsically bad or AI-generated; repeated/contextually unsuitable usage is a review candidate.

## Limitations and how to validate
Sources are standards plus selected editorial guidance, not a random sample or an A/B test. Publication-date and product-law applicability may change. For production, build a 20–30-item **licensed or user-provided** set of real drafts, have at least two qualified Japanese-language editors rate tone/action clarity/claim fidelity, and reconcile substantial disagreements. Keep exact customer data confidential.

## Mode-specific evaluation scenarios

### Consumer product LP
- **Scenario:** a foldable shopping bag is described using only known product features.
- **Question:** Does the headline identify the practical action without adding claims of strength or environmental impact?
- **Fail:** introducing `防水`, `最軽量`, `CO2削減` without verified source.
- **Evidence to request:** product specification, manufacturer statement, actual catalog page.
- **Editor response:** preserve verified feature and disclose the missing qualification outside final copy.

### Discount banner
- **Scenario:** marketer supplies current price but not the prior price history.
- **Question:** Can the copy use `50%OFF` or `通常価格` with validated comparative basis?
- **Fail:** keeping the discount assertion but removing only reference price.
- **Evidence to request:** price history, reference-price basis, eligible SKUs, exact offer period.
- **Editor response:** HOLD offer claim rather than invent numbers or safe-looking discount adjectives.

### Beauty ad
- **Scenario:** cosmetic benefit asserted from a Korean source script.
- **Question:** Is the Japan product category known and is the exact efficacy permissible in context?
- **Fail:** changing `治る` to `改善をサポート` then declaring it legally safe without classification.
- **Evidence to request:** category/approval, product labeling, substantiation and visuals.
- **Editor response:** HOLD the efficacy statement; use confirmed composition/usage language only if present.

### Sponsored social post
- **Scenario:** creator receives product compensation, but advertiser control and platform disclosure method are not specified.
- **Question:** Can consumers identify business-directed advertising at viewing time?
- **Fail:** asserting `#PR` is guaranteed sufficient regardless of size or placement.
- **Evidence to request:** contract, advertiser involvement, creative mockup and platform.
- **Editor response:** flag disclosure review; no blanket automatic legal approval.

### B2B outreach email
- **Scenario:** a first email to a company not previously contacted.
- **Question:** Does the writer pretend there is a prior relationship or referral?
- **Fail:** `いつもお世話になっております` plus a fabricated shared colleague or meeting.
- **Evidence to request:** real sender, target contact, referral, relationship history.
- **Editor response:** use respectful initial approach and clear reason, not fake intimacy.

### Request follow-up
- **Scenario:** buyer has not supplied a due date.
- **Question:** Is the action specific without inserting a 'today by 5 PM' deadline?
- **Fail:** inventing a deadline to improve email directness.
- **Evidence to request:** real deadline and recipient availability.
- **Editor response:** ask availability or leave a true no-deadline request.

### Promotional mailing
- **Scenario:** a newsletter body looks natural and is already edited.
- **Question:** Has actual send eligibility, sender data and functioning opt-out been verified?
- **Fail:** editorial PASS presented as send eligibility.
- **Evidence to request:** consent or relevant exception, sender information and unsubscribe implementation.
- **Editor response:** HOLD-SEND separately from writing quality if not verified.

### Quality evaluation design
- Conduct blinded A/B language review with two or more Japanese editors when possible.
- Score separately for **semantic fidelity**, **keigo appropriateness**, **reader clarity**, **brand voice**, **commercial claim integrity**, and **needed action clarity**.
- A legally risky phrase is reviewed independently of naturalness and engagement forecasts.
- Include controlled false-positive cases (e.g., established long-term client with `いつもお世話になっております`, scientific article quoting `No.1` as a criticism).
- Keep source originals licensed or user-owned; avoid reproducing online articles or private client details in a public repository.
