# Synthetic advertising examples — demonstrations only

## A. Software SaaS performance claim — HOLD
Facts supplied: project management app supports task lists and due date fields. No usage data or approved ranking.

**Before:** `業界No.1！生産性が必ず2倍になるタスク管理アプリ。`
**Decision:** HOLD-CLAIM — `業界No.1`, `必ず2倍` not supported. Do NOT invent a survey.
**Candidate after:** `タスクと期限を、ひとつの画面で管理。`
**Check:** This requires product UI supporting a *single screen*. If not verified, instead: `タスクと期限を管理できるアプリ。`

## B. Commerce offer — HOLD
Brief gives product only; lacks historical price/discount deadline.
**Before:** `今日だけ50%OFF！通常価格19,800円→9,900円。`
