# Synthetic marketing LP example (not real brand data)

**Facts provided for this exercise**: fictional company sells a *reusable, foldable shopping bag* in two sizes. The source establishes no measurements, water-resistance, environmental-impact figures, sales records, or price.

### Before (generic)
`毎日のショッピング体験を革新する、画期的なソリューション。究極の利便性をあなたに。今すぐ体験しましょう。`

### After (grounded)
**見出し:** `買い物が終わったら、折りたたんで持ち帰る。`

**本文:** `くり返し使えるショッピングバッグ。サイズは2種類です。`

**CTA:** `サイズを見る`

**Why:** product category, folding and size are from the brief; no extra weight/strength/performance promise. Do not add `軽量`, `防水`, `地球にやさしい` unless substantiated. "買い物が終わったら" is usage framing, not an invented customer quote. If the shopping context isn't supported by the product, revise accordingly.

**Truth gate:** PASS in this *hypothetical* exercise. Real copy requires actual spec proof and review of store/CTA destination.
